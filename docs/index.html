🎮 Twitcher

Automated Twitch Stream Control Center

📥 Download Twitcher.exe

Requires VLC media player installed. No Python needed.

▶ Auto-Play

Starts playing your last-watched stream the moment you launch the app — no login required.

📊 Real-time Analytics

Viewer momentum tracking, SullyGnome analytics, and stream quality scoring.

💬 Built-in Chat

Twitch IRC chat with Latin→Cyrillic transliteration for Russian users.

⏭ Next Stream Prediction

Suggests the best channel to switch to if the current stream ends.

📺 Live Followed Channels

Shows all your followed channels that are currently live with viewer counts.
